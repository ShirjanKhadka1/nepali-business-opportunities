# Sector: Tourism, done properly

**Why Nepal:** mountains, culture, and heritage that cannot be copied
elsewhere. The opportunity is less in "another hotel" and more in the
services around a visit that are currently fragmented or low-quality.

**Who pays:** international visitors, domestic tourists, and the businesses
that serve them (guides, transport, food, gear, wellness).

**Main barriers:**
- Seasonality — revenue concentrated in a few months.
- Infrastructure and safety standards that visitors actually check.
- Dependence on a few source markets and on flight access.
- Environmental and community impact if growth is unmanaged.

**What to verify first:**
1. Seasonality curve for the specific location/activity (not "Nepal tourism").
2. Real booking / occupancy data for comparable operators.
3. Permits, insurance, and safety requirements for that activity, in writing.
