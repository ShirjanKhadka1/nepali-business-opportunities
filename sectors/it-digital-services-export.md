# Sector: IT & digital services for export

**Why Nepal (structural reason, not a statistic):** English-speaking,
technically trained graduates; time-zone overlap with Asia-Pacific and
partial overlap with Europe; labour costs below the markets served; and a
growing diaspora that can open doors abroad. These are reasons to
investigate, not proof of a market — verify demand first.

**Who pays:** foreign clients for software, design, data, QA, support, or
content services; or Nepali firms buying the same services locally.

**Main barriers:**
- Trust and track record with foreign clients (portfolio + references matter more than price).
- Payment, contracts, and FX for cross-border work.
- Power and internet reliability for delivery deadlines.
- Talent retention against remote hiring by foreign firms.

**What to verify first:**
1. 5 real foreign or local clients who would pay, and at what rate.
2. Payment route (bank, wallet, platform) and its fees/timing, in writing.
3. 3 competitors with their prices and their client proof.

**Scorecard:** use `framework/scorecard.md`. Do not rank this sector until
its scorecard is filled with cited evidence.
