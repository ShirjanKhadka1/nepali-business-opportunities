# Sector: Agriculture & food processing

**Why Nepal:** Nepal already grows the raw material. The value that leaves
is in processing, packaging, branding, and distribution — the steps that
turn a commodity into a product with a margin.

**Who pays:** domestic consumers, retailers, hotels, export buyers for
processed/packaged Nepali products.

**Main barriers:**
- Cold chain, storage, and transport from farm to processor.
- Quality consistency and food-safety compliance.
- Working capital tied up in seasonal inventory.
- Price competition from imports.

**What to verify first:**
1. Farm-gate price vs processed-product price for one specific product.
2. Cold-chain / transport cost for that product's actual route.
3. Food-safety / labelling requirements for that product, in writing.
